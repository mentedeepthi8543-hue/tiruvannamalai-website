What are Pancha Bhoota Sthalams?

The Pancha Bhoota Sthalams are five ancient Shiva temples representing the five fundamental elements of nature.

ॐ

According to Hindu philosophy, the universe and the human body are composed of five natural elements known as Pancha Bhootas.

Lord Shiva is worshipped in a distinct elemental form at each temple. These sacred temples are located in Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh.
