01

Sri Ramana Ashramam

Sri Ramana Ashramam is one of the most peaceful and spiritually important places in Tiruvannamalai. It is dedicated to Bhagavan Sri Ramana Maharshi, who taught self-enquiry and inner silence.

Devotees visit the ashram for meditation, prayer, spiritual guidance and peace of mind.

Famous For: Meditation, Ramana Maharshi Samadhi and peaceful surroundings

Distance: Around 2 km from Arunachaleswarar Temple

Best Time: Morning and evening

02

Virupaksha Cave

Virupaksha Cave is a sacred meditation cave situated on Arunachala Hill. Sri Ramana Maharshi stayed and meditated here for many years.

Visitors trekking on Arunachala Hill visit this cave to experience silence, meditation and spiritual peace.

Famous For: Ramana Maharshi meditation cave

Location: Arunachala Hill trekking path

Best Time: Early morning

03

Skandashramam

Skandashramam is another important spiritual location on Arunachala Hill. Sri Ramana Maharshi lived here with his mother for several years.

The location offers a beautiful view of Tiruvannamalai town, Arunachala Hill and Arunachaleswarar Temple.

Famous For: Meditation, hill view and Ramana Maharshi history

Location: Arunachala Hill

Best Time: Morning trekking hours

04

Yogi Ramsuratkumar Ashram

Yogi Ramsuratkumar Ashram is dedicated to Yogi Ramsuratkumar, lovingly known as the Godchild of Tiruvannamalai.

The ashram provides a peaceful devotional atmosphere with a spacious prayer hall and spiritual activities.

Famous For: Yogi Ramsuratkumar, prayer hall and spiritual peace

Location: Near Tiruvannamalai town

Best Time: Morning or evening

📍 Open Route Map
05

Sathanur Dam

Sathanur Dam is a popular natural attraction near Tiruvannamalai. It is surrounded by hills, water, greenery and peaceful landscapes.

Visitors can enjoy scenic views, gardens and a relaxing atmosphere with family and friends.

Famous For: Dam view, nature, gardens and relaxation

Distance: Around 30 km from Tiruvannamalai

Best Time: Morning to evening

📍 Open Route Map
06

Pachaiamman Temple

Pachaiamman Temple is a famous goddess temple near Tiruvannamalai. Devotees visit the temple seeking blessings, protection and family welfare.

The temple is closely connected with local traditions, village culture and devotional worship.

Famous For: Goddess worship, blessings and peaceful surroundings

Location: Near Tiruvannamalai

Best Time: Morning and evening
