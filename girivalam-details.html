Tiruvannamalai Girivalam is a sacred clockwise walking pilgrimage around Arunachala Hill. Devotees begin from Arunachaleswarar Temple, worship at the eight directional Ashta Lingams and return to the temple after completing approximately 14 kilometres.

The route includes drinking-water points, rest areas, Annadanam centres, public toilets, medical support, shops and important spiritual locations.

📍 Starting Point: Arunachaleswarar Temple

🚶 Average Walking Time: 3.5–5 hours depending on breaks and crowd levels

🌄 Recommended Time: Early morning or evening

🕉️ Eight Ashta Lingams

The Ashta Lingams represent the eight directions surrounding Arunachala Hill. Pilgrims traditionally visit them in order while completing Girivalam.

🕉️ 1. Indra Lingam

Eastern Direction

Indra Lingam is the first Ashta Lingam visited during Girivalam. It is associated with Lord Indra, King of the Devas.

Devotees pray here for success, prosperity, confidence and removal of obstacles.

📍 View Route

🔥 2. Agni Lingam

South-East Direction

Agni Lingam represents divine fire, transformation and purification.

Devotees worship here for courage, positive energy, spiritual strength and removal of negativity.

📍 View Route

⚖️ 3. Yama Lingam

Southern Direction

Yama Lingam is associated with Lord Yama, the deity connected with justice, discipline and righteous life.

Devotees pray for health, long life, courage and protection from fear.

📍 View Route

🛡️ 4. Niruthi Lingam

South-West Direction

Niruthi Lingam represents protection from negative forces and difficulties.

Devotees pray here for peace, stability, safety and spiritual protection.

📍 View Route

🌊 5. Varuna Lingam

Western Direction

Varuna Lingam is connected with Lord Varuna and the purifying quality of water.

Devotees seek emotional balance, family harmony, mental peace and purity.

📍 View Route

🌬️ 6. Vayu Lingam

North-West Direction

Vayu Lingam is associated with the wind element and life energy.

Devotees worship for physical health, breathing wellness, peace of mind and spiritual growth.

📍 View Route

💰 7. Kubera Lingam

Northern Direction

Kubera Lingam is associated with Lord Kubera, the deity of wealth and prosperity.

Devotees pray for financial stability, business growth, prosperity and good fortune.

📍 View Route

🕉️ 8. Esanya Lingam

North-East Direction

Esanya Lingam is the final Ashta Lingam visited before returning to Arunachaleswarar Temple.

Devotees pray for wisdom, spiritual knowledge, devotion, inner peace and guidance toward moksha.

💧 Pilgrim Facilities

Facilities may vary according to the location, crowd level, festival arrangements and time of day.

💧

Drinking Water

Water points and temporary distribution stalls are available at different locations.

🪑

Rest Points

Shaded seating and temporary resting areas help pilgrims take short breaks.

🍛

Annadanam Centres

Free food may be distributed by temples, ashrams, trusts and volunteers.

🚻

Public Toilets

Public sanitation facilities are available at selected places along the route.

🅿️

Parking

Use authorised parking areas and follow temporary traffic restrictions during busy days.

🚑

Medical Support

Medical camps and emergency support may be provided during Pournami and major festivals.

🌤️ Best Time for Girivalam

🌄 Early Morning: Approximately 4:30 AM–8:00 AM

🌇 Evening: Approximately 5:00 PM–9:00 PM

☀️ Hot Weather: Avoid peak afternoon heat and take regular breaks.

🌧️ Rainy Weather: Carry rain protection and wear suitable non-slip footwear where permitted.

🌕 Pournami: Expect very large crowds, traffic diversions and longer walking times.

📜 Girivalam Do's & Don'ts

✅ What to Do

  • • Begin the journey early and check the weather.
  • • Carry drinking water and stay hydrated.
  • • Wear comfortable and modest clothing.
  • • Follow the clockwise Girivalam route.
  • • Visit the Ashta Lingams in sequence.
  • • Follow police and volunteer instructions.
  • • Take rest when feeling tired or unwell.
  • • Respect temples, pilgrims and local customs.

❌ What Not to Do

  • • Do not litter along the sacred route.
  • • Do not walk during extreme afternoon heat.
  • • Do not block roads or emergency vehicles.
  • • Do not disturb other pilgrims.
  • • Do not carry unnecessary heavy luggage.
  • • Do not ignore traffic while crossing roads.
  • • Do not consume alcohol or tobacco products.
  • • Do not leave children or elderly people alone.

🚑 Medical & Emergency Assistance

In an emergency, move to a safe location and contact the appropriate service. Share your exact Girivalam location or nearest Lingam with the responder.
